I used to keep two little blogs here, Tech Bytes and Life Bytes, and then like most personal sites it quietly went dormant and GoDaddy started showing a parking page on my own name. So this is the reboot.

The split is the same as before because it still makes sense to me. Tech Bytes is the engineering log, the stuff I had to figure out at work or on side projects and want to be able to point at later. Life Bytes is everything else, the projects that don't have a CI pipeline.

The plan is small posts, written when there's actually something to say. I've been doing more with automation lately, so some of the writing here will start life as notes and highlights that get pulled together weekly, and the longer pieces will land here where a LinkedIn post can point at them instead of me trying to cram an idea into a caption.

No promises on cadence. One byte at a time.
