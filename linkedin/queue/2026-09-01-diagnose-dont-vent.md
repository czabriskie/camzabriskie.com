I keep seeing the same kind of post from people job searching. Out of work this many months, sent this many applications, nothing back. True, and exhausting, and also not really something anyone reading it can do anything with.

I've been thinking about what the more useful version looks like while working on ClimbIQ. Something closer to, I compared these postings against my actual experience, found five things I'm missing, and I've started closing a couple of them. Same situation, but now there's something for someone to actually respond to.

Not sure if that's obvious advice or just a framing nobody says out loud under stress.
